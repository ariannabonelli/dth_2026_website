# What are the disadvantages of open-source software?

## Disadvantages of open-source software

Despite its strengths, OSS also has clear disadvantages. 
 
Because OSS often relies on volunteers, the effort can be inconsistent, which may delay the support a project needs to run well. Another aspect is related to reachability. It can be hard to reach the right developers when support is needed, since there is no single company to contact (Mathpati, 2023). 

### When free comes at a cost

- Security is another concern. Because the code is public, attackers can study it for weaknesses, and a small team may not have the time to fix them quickly; it's important to keep the software up to date and follow best practices (Mathpati, 2023). 
 
- Documentation also deserves attention. Even when a project has a core team, the instructions are sometimes unclear to users without technical knowledge, which makes the software harder to adopt (Harness, 2024). 

- A related problem is limited functionality. OSS may not offer the same range as proprietary software, especially in niche areas (Mathpati, 2023). From a student’s perspective, a project may require specific tools. A free tool might exist, but a paid professional tool often offers more advanced options.
